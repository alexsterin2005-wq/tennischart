TennisChart Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026
Publisher: BaselineBook LLC

TennisChart is a tennis match charting app. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. TennisChart has no accounts, no login, and no servers. Everything you enter — player names, scores, notes — stays on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't send your data anywhere.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell or share anything, because we don't have anything.

Sharing stats

When you tap "Copy stats," the app creates an image on your device and places it on your clipboard. Where you paste it is up to you.

Children

TennisChart does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
