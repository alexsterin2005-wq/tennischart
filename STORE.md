**Description:**
TennisChart.io is the match charting app for tennis parents who aren't coaches. Tap along from the sideline, watch each player's chance to win move with every point, and send the coach a pro-style stat sheet.

Three taps a point: serve in or fault, who won, how. Tap the wrong thing? Undo. Ball rolled on? Replay. Lost track of the score? Tap the scoreboard and fix it. You'll never get stuck.

LIVE WIN CHANCE
Add both players' UTR before the match and TennisChart.io shows each player's chance to win, updated after every point. Big points like break points move it more than early points. It runs entirely on your phone. Prefer not to see it? Turn it off in one tap.

PRO-LEVEL STATS
First serve percentage, points won on first and second serve, break points saved and converted, winners and errors by shot, rally length, and key moments. Filter by set. Tap Copy stats and paste a clean image straight into a text to the coach.

## App Review notes (1.1)
```
WHAT'S NEW IN 1.1
Live win odds, a redesigned interface, and the privacy policy inside the app (Setup screen, bottom link "Privacy policy"). No new permissions, no accounts, no network features.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached, recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS. It begins at app launch and shows: entering two player names and UTR ratings, choosing who serves, charting points (serve in/fault, who won, how, shot), the live win odds updating, undo, the stats screen, Copy stats, Sun mode, and the in-app privacy policy. The app has no accounts, login, user-generated content shared with others, or paid features.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
TennisChart helps parents of junior tennis players chart a match from the sideline and share the stats with their child's coach. New in 1.1: optional live win odds. If both players' UTR ratings are entered, the app estimates each player's chance to win and updates it after every point using standard tennis probability math, calculated entirely on the device. It is informational only: there is no betting, wagering, or money of any kind in the app.
Native features: haptic feedback on every point and game (Capacitor Haptics), status bar styling, offline storage of the match on the device, and the system clipboard/share for the stats image.

3. HOW TO USE IT
- Setup: type two names. Optionally type each player's UTR (a public tennis rating, e.g. 10.25) to enable live odds. Tap Start match, then pick who serves first.
- Live: tap In or Fault, then who won the point, then how (Winner, Forced error, Unforced error), then the shot (or Skip). The scoreboard and the green/red win odds update after each point.
- Tap the scoreboard to fix the score. Undo, Let/Replay, and Note are on the bottom bar. The sun button switches to high-contrast Sun mode.
- Stats: tap Stats, then Copy stats to put a stat sheet image on the clipboard.
- Live odds can be turned off on the Setup screen ("Live odds off").

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network requests. No analytics, ads, accounts, or tracking. Fonts and all code are bundled in the app.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. No gambling or wagering: win odds are a statistic shown to the parent, nothing can be bet or purchased. "UTR" refers to the publicly visible Universal Tennis Rating number that the user types in by hand; the app does not connect to UTR.
```
