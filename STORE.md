**Description:**
TennisChart is the match charting app for parents who aren't coaches.

Sit courtside and tap along. Serve in or fault, who won, how. That's it. If you tap the wrong thing, hit Undo. If a ball rolls onto the court, hit Replay. If you lose track of the score, tap the scoreboard and fix it. You'll never get stuck.

When the match is over (or whenever you want), tap Stats and see the match the way the ATP shows it: first serve percentage, points won on first and second serve, break points saved and converted, winners and errors by shot, rally length. Filter by set. Tap Copy stats and paste a clean image straight into a text to the coach.

WHAT YOU GET
• Three taps per point, big buttons, thumb-friendly layout
• Undo any serve or point, replay a point, fix the score, add a let
• Break point, set point, and match point alerts on the scoreboard
• Ad or no-ad scoring, 10-point match tiebreak or full third set
• ATP-style stats: serve, return, points, by shot, by rally length
• Key moments: every break point and set point, with what happened
• Timestamped notes during the match
• One-tap stats image for texting to a coach
• Sun mode for bright courts
• No account. No login. Nothing leaves your phone.

Made by a D1 tennis player and coach for the parents on the sideline.
